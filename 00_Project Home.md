# Kenya in Data

> **Mission:** Move public conversation in Kenya from vibes to evidence by building a trustworthy, reproducible, and visually compelling data publication machine.

## Operating Model

**Kenya in Data** is the institution. **Research projects** are self-contained evidence workspaces. **Publications** are the individual charts, briefs, threads, and datasets produced by those projects.

`institution → research project → one or more publications`

## Core Editorial Axiom

> **Show first. Interpret second.**

We do not tell people whom to support. We add the **denominator**, the **deflator**, and the **missing context** needed to make public data clear and useful.
