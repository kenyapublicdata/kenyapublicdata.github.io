# Kenya in Data Inbox

Use this directory as the rapid-capture staging ground for:
- New statistical releases and PDF reports (e.g. from KNBS, CBK, National Treasury, Controller of Budget).
- Specific public claims made by politicians or officials to be investigated.
- Ideas for new graphics or research questions.
- Unvetted raw datasets, CSVs, or web links.

## Processing Rules
1. **Never delete raw capture without routing:** Move processed items to either a new `KID-###` publication folder, `Indicators/`, or archive them under `Archive/` with a timestamp.
2. **Tag incoming items:** Note the source, retrieval date, and initial hypothesis or question.
