# 2026-08-28 — KID-002 Publication Release: Uhuru vs Ruto Debt Trajectories

**Kenya in Data** released its second flagship publication:
- **Title:** *Uhuru vs Ruto: Kenya's Public Debt in Their First Four Fiscal-Year Windows* (KID-002)
- **Live URL:** https://kenyapublicdata.github.io/publications/kid-002/
- **Project:** PRJ-001 Kenya Public Debt
- **Primary Sources:** National Treasury (*Annual Public Debt Reports 2016/17, 2024/25, June 2026 Monthly Bulletin*), KNBS (*Consumer Price Indices 2013–2026*).

### Key Takeaways:
- **Debt Growth Rate:** Uhuru grew the debt stock 2.7× faster (+132.7% nominal, +75.2% real vs +48.5% nominal, +19.1% real under the Ruto window).
- **Nominal Increase:** Ruto window added +KSh 4.252T on a 4.6× larger starting base vs +KSh 2.513T under Uhuru.
- **Debt / GDP:** Uhuru outpaced GDP (+15.1 pp), whereas the Ruto window endpoint was flat (~68.5%).
- **Interest Squeeze:** Total interest payments as % of ordinary revenue doubled from 20.8% (Uhuru endpoint) to 40.8% (Ruto era).
