Civic Observatory & Public Data Initiative

Moving Kenya's public conversation from vibes to evidence.

Kenya generates enormous amounts of public data, but public debate often runs on political noise and unverified claims. Kenya in Data turns scattered, complex public records into clear, trustworthy, and visually compelling evidence.

Editorial Axiom: Show first. Interpret second. We do not tell citizens whom to support. We add the denominator, the deflator, and the verifiable context needed to make public data clear and useful.

How It Works

The Publication Pipeline

How primary official records become trustworthy public knowledge.

01 / DISCOVER & VERIFY

Primary Sources Only

We extract data directly from official statutory reports, censuses, statistical bulletins, and audited records with exact page and table citations.

02 / CONTEXTUALIZE

Add the Denominator

Numbers without context mislead. We adjust for population scale (per capita), purchasing power (inflation deflators), and national economic size (% of GDP).

03 / OPEN ACCESS

100% Free & Open

Every graphic, methodology, and cleaned dataset is freely published in machine-readable formats for journalists, researchers, and citizens.

Editorial Standards & Neutrality

The rules that safeguard our credibility and public trust.

Non-Partisan Commitment

We do not endorse political parties, candidates, or policy factions. Timelines serve as historical landmarks, not causal rankings.

Granular Provenance

Every statistic must have an explicit source citation, table reference, and retrieval date from official statutory publishers.

Transparent Corrections

If an error is discovered or an official agency revises historical data, corrections are prominently logged with original and updated values.
