Uhuru vs Ruto: Kenya's Public Debt in Their First Four Fiscal-Year Windows

A head-to-head empirical comparison: Uhuru grew the debt stock 2.7× faster (+132.7% nominal, +75.2% real) and outpaced GDP. The Ruto window added more nominal shillings (+KSh 4.25T vs +KSh 2.51T) on a 4.6× larger base, while interest payments doubled to 40.8% of revenue.
