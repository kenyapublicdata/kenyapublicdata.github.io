CIVIC OBSERVATORY & PUBLIC DATA MACHINE

Moving Kenya's public conversation from vibes to evidence.

Kenya in Data is an independent civic data initiative dedicated to building trustworthy, reproducible, and visually compelling evidence on public finances, devolution, and macroeconomics.

Core Editorial Axiom: Show first. Interpret second. We do not tell citizens whom to support. We add the denominator, the deflator, and the verifiable context needed to make public data clear and useful.

Operational Philosophy

How We Work

Our operating rules ensure high epistemic standards and public trust.

PILLAR 01

Denominators & Deflators First

We never present large nominal sums in isolation over time. Every headline metric is paired with inflation adjustments (constant shillings) and national scale (% of GDP or revenue).

PILLAR 02

Granular Provenance

Every published number is traceable to its primary source: exact report title, publication date, table number, and page number from official publishers (Treasury, KNBS, CBK, OAG).

PILLAR 03

100% Open & Reproducible

Data is a public good. All charting code, Python themes, and tidy datasets (CSV/Excel) are openly published for citizens, journalists, and researchers to verify.

Editorial Constitution

Epistemic Neutrality & Public Good Pledge

Kenya in Data operates as an evidence-grounded civic institution. We do not endorse political figures, parties, or policy factions. Timeline shading across administrations serves exclusively as a historical landmark, not a partisan causal claim. Corrections are transparently logged with original and revised series disclosed.
