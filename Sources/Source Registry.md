# Official Source Registry

This registry tracks Kenya's primary official data providers, statutory publication series, canonical URLs, publication schedules, and reliability notes.

> **Provenance Standard:** Under Kenya in Data's [[Operations/Data and Analysis Standards|Standards]], all metrics must originate from Tier 1 Primary Sources (official statutory reports, gazettes, open data portals, or multilateral datasets) rather than secondary commentary or social media aggregation.

## 2. Discovery & Aggregation Layer (Third-Party Channels)

Social media channels and independent data curators are valuable for **topic discovery and finding trending public discussions**, but their posts must always be cross-referenced back to the primary statutory sources listed above.

| Channel / Account | Focus Areas | Relationship to Primary Sources |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Kenya Data & Stats** (`@Stats_Kenya` / `statskenya.co.ke`) | Demographics, county socioeconomic rankings, election voting patterns, public sector wages, social indicators | **Third-party Aggregator / Storyteller:** Visualizes secondary extracts derived primarily from KNBS (Census/KDHS/GCP), OCOB, IEBC, and CBK. |
| **KNBS Official** (`@KNBStats`) | Official statistical release alerts, survey launches, census announcements | **Primary Publisher Handle:** Official Twitter/X voice of the national statistical bureau. |
| **Central Bank of Kenya** (`@CBKKenya`) | Weekly bulletins, monetary policy committee (MPC) decisions, exchange rates | **Primary Publisher Handle:** Official communication channel of the central bank. |

## 3. Ingestion & Provenance Guidelines

1. **Never use secondary news reports or social infographics for quantitative claims** when the underlying primary bulletin, gazette notice, or raw dataset is accessible.
2. **Store raw downloaded PDFs/CSVs under `Data/Raw/` or `Sources/Documents/`** with a standard naming convention:
   `[YEAR]_[INSTITUTION]_[TOPIC]_[PERIOD].[ext]`  
   *Example:* `2024_KNBS_EconomicSurvey.pdf` or `2024_OCOB_CountyBIRR_Q3.pdf`.
