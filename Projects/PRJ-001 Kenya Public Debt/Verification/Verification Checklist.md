# Verification Checklist

## Sources

- [ ] Every used number maps to a registered source, table, page, sheet, or series.
- [ ] Primary official sources are used where available.
- [ ] Editions, revisions, retrieval dates, and units are recorded.
- [ ] Conflicting official values have been reconciled or disclosed.

## Data and Calculations

- [ ] Raw data are preserved unchanged.
- [ ] Transformation steps are reproducible.
- [ ] Units and frequencies are consistent.
- [ ] Missing values, revisions, and breaks in series are handled explicitly.
- [ ] Deflator and base period are correct.
- [ ] Debt-to-GDP numerator and denominator periods align.
- [ ] Calculations have received an independent second check.

## Interpretation

- [ ] Nominal, real, ratio, and burden measures are not conflated.
- [ ] Administration bands are not presented as proof of causation.
- [ ] Exchange-rate effects on external debt are explained.
- [ ] Important exclusions and limitations are visible.
- [ ] The headline is supported by the evidence.

## Figure and Publication

- [ ] Axes, labels, units, dates, and legends are accurate.
- [ ] Visual scales and comparisons do not mislead.
- [ ] Source footer and methodology reference are present.
- [ ] Figure, caption, and published data agree.
- [ ] Exact released files are preserved.
- [ ] Correction contact or process is available.
