## Explicit exclusions

- No May 2026 debt-to-GDP ratio is published because this release has no matched May 2026 GDP denominator.
- No total-expenditure denominator or debt-service/expenditure calculation is included in the dataset or article.
- No current IMF/World Bank debt-distress classification is claimed.
- No June 2026 total is constructed by combining domestic and external observations from different months.
- No recipient-level allocation of domestic interest is claimed because the source reports year-end holdings by category, not annual interest receipts by holder.
- No commercial-bank interest receipt is described as profit.
