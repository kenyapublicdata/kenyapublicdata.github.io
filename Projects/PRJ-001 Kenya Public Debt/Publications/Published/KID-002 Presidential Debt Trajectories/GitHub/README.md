# Kenya's Public Debt: A 24-Year Historical Analysis (2002–2026)

[![Package ID: KID-002](https://img.shields.io/badge/Package-KID--002-blue.svg)](../../package.yml)
[![Licence: CC BY 4.0](https://img.shields.io/badge/Licence-CC_BY_4.0-lightgrey.svg)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
[![Status: Published](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-Published-success.svg)](../../KID-002%20Presidential%20Debt%20Trajectories.md)

An open Kenya in Data publication package covering Kenya's complete 24-year public debt trajectory across the administrations of **Mwai Kibaki (2002–2013)**, **Uhuru Kenyatta (2013–2022)**, and **William Ruto (2022–2026)**, featuring an in-depth **Early-Term Accession Deep-Dive (~First 3 Years)** adjusted for inflation using official **KNBS date-matched Consumer Price Index (CPI)** data.

## 🎯 24-Year Core Historical Trajectory

| Metric / Regime | Mwai Kibaki (2002–2013) | Uhuru Kenyatta (2013–2022) | William Ruto (2022–2026) |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Nominal Debt Stock** | KSh 629.5B ➔ KSh 1.894T | KSh 1.894T ➔ KSh 8.761T | KSh 8.761T ➔ KSh 11.814T (➔ 12.9T) |
| **Debt-to-GDP Ratio** | 📉 **64.0% ➔ 42.1%** *(-21.9 pp)* | 📈 **42.1% ➔ 68.7%** *(+26.6 pp)* | **68.7% ➔ 67.8%** *(-0.9 pp / Flat)* |
| **Borrowing Model** | Concessional Multilateral (IDA/AfDB) | Commercial Eurobonds & China Exim SGR | IMF/WB Refinancing & Domestic T-Bonds |
| **Debt Service / Revenue** | **23.0% – 28.2%** | **28.2% ➔ 47.9%** | **47.9% ➔ 71.2%** *(KSh 1.722 Trillion)* |
| **Fiscal Squeeze** | Low servicing burden; tax revenue +324% | Rising commercial principal & coupons | Domestic interest eats 32.1% rev @ 13.0% yield |

## 🔍 Early-Term Accession Subset (The First ~3 Years)

| Administration | Observation Window | Start Debt | End Debt | Nominal Growth | Real Growth (CPI Deflated)* | Debt-to-GDP |
|---|:---:|---:|---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Mwai Kibaki** | Dec 2002 ➔ Jun 2005 (~2.5 Yrs) | KSh 629.5B | KSh 750.0B | **+19.1%** | 📉 **-10.8%** | **64.0% ➔ 55.6%** *(-8.4 pp)* |
| **Uhuru Kenyatta** | Jun 2013 ➔ Jun 2016 (3 Yrs) | KSh 1,894.1B | KSh 3,611.3B | **+90.7%** | 📈 **+56.8%** | **42.1% ➔ 54.8%** *(+12.7 pp)* |
| **William Ruto** | Jun 2022 ➔ Jun 2025 (3 Yrs) | KSh 8,761.0B | KSh 11,814.5B | **+34.8%** | 📈 **+15.1%** | **68.7% ➔ 67.8%** *(-0.9 pp)* |

*\*Deflators: Kibaki CPI +33.5%; Uhuru CPI +21.6%; Ruto CPI +17.2%.*
