## LinkedIn

**Kenya's public debt: a current baseline**

The latest complete debt total in the CBK June 2026 bulletin is **KSh 12.896 trillion at May 2026**. Domestic debt accounts for **56.1%**, while external debt accounts for **43.9%**.

The latest complete fiscal-year picture comes from the National Treasury. In FY 2024/25, total debt service was **KSh 1.722 trillion**, equivalent to **71.2% of ordinary revenue**. Domestic interest alone was **KSh 776.3 billion**, or **32.1% of ordinary revenue**.

At June 2025, the Treasury reported nominal debt at **67.8% of GDP** and present-value debt at **63.7% of GDP**, compared with the **55% present-value anchor** in the cited consolidation of the Public Finance Management Act.

The dates matter: May 2026 provides the latest complete debt stock, while June 2025 provides the matched fiscal ratios. We do not calculate a May 2026 debt-to-GDP ratio without a matching GDP denominator.
