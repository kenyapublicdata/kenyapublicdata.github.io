## Primary caption

**Kenya's public debt: a current baseline**

Kenya's public and publicly guaranteed debt stood at **KSh 12.896 trillion in May 2026**, the latest month in the CBK's June bulletin with complete domestic and external totals.

Domestic debt accounted for **56.1%**; external debt accounted for **43.9%**.

In FY 2024/25, debt service reached **KSh 1.722 trillion**—equivalent to **71.2% of ordinary revenue**.

The dates differ by metric because the most recent debt stock and the latest complete fiscal-year ratios are published on different schedules. All figures are provisional.

**Data:** Central Bank of Kenya, *Monthly Economic Indicators: June 2026*, Table 7.1, p. 22; National Treasury, *Annual Public Debt Management Report FY 2024/25*, Table 4, p. 30.
