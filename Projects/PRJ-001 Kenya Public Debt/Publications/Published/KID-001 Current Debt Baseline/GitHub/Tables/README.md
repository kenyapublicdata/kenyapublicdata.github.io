# Downloadable publication tables

These CSV files are reader-friendly extracts from the canonical [KID-001 publication dataset](../../Data/KID001_current_debt_baseline.csv). They are provided for reuse in spreadsheets, reporting, teaching and independent checking.

| File | Coverage | Primary source |
|---|---|---|
| [`current_debt_snapshot_may_2026.csv`](current_debt_snapshot_may_2026.csv) | Total, domestic and external debt | CBK, Table 7.1, p. 22 |
| [`debt_components_june_2025.csv`](debt_components_june_2025.csv) | Domestic instruments and external creditor categories | National Treasury, Tables 5 and 10, pp. 34 and 45 |
| [`debt_service_fy2024_25.csv`](debt_service_fy2024_25.csv) | Interest and principal components | National Treasury, Table 4, p. 30 |
| [`domestic_interest_fy2024_25.csv`](domestic_interest_fy2024_25.csv) | Domestic interest and charges by instrument | National Treasury, Table 9, p. 44 |
| [`domestic_debt_holders_june_2025.csv`](domestic_debt_holders_june_2025.csv) | Domestic debt stock by holder category | National Treasury, Table 6, p. 36 |
| [`weighted_average_interest_rates_fy2024_25.csv`](weighted_average_interest_rates_fy2024_25.csv) | Domestic, external and total portfolio rates | National Treasury, Section 9.3 and Figure 17, p. 68 |

## Important interpretation notes

- May 2026 and June 2025 values come from different official releases.
- No May 2026 debt-to-GDP ratio is included.
- Domestic principal excludes routine Treasury-bill redemptions.
- Holder shares describe the debt stock at June 2025, not annual interest receipts.
- Values are provisional and may be revised by the source institution.

For transformations and complete provenance, use the canonical dataset, [data dictionary](../../Data/data_dictionary.csv) and [citation ledger](../../Sources/citation_ledger.md).
