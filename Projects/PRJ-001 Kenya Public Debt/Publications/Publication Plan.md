# Publication Plan

## Proposed First Release

**Working ID:** `KID-001`  
**Working title:** Kenya's Public Debt: The Current Stock, Composition and Servicing Burden

Draft publication package: [KID-001 Current Debt Baseline](Drafts/KID-001%20Current%20Debt%20Baseline/00_Package%20Home.md).

The first release will use the verified FY 2020/21–FY 2024/25 Treasury series, the June 2025 fiscal-year baseline and the complete May 2026 monthly update. It will focus on the current debt stock, domestic/external composition and the debt-service burden.

The longer 2002–2026 administration comparison remains a follow-on release pending reconstruction and verification of the annual source series.

## Possible Follow-on Releases

- Domestic versus external debt composition.
- Exchange-rate revaluation and the shilling value of external debt.
- Debt service relative to government revenue.
- A methodology brief and downloadable public dataset.

Final sequencing depends on data continuity and verification. Do not announce an output before its minimum evidence requirements are met.
