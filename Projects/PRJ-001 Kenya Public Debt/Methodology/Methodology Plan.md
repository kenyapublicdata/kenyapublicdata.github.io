# Methodology Plan

## Decisions Required

1. Select the canonical public-debt definition and longest comparable official series.
2. Select observation dates and rules for mapping observations to administrations.
3. Choose a constant-price base period and primary deflator.
4. Define a CPI-based sensitivity check.
5. Select the GDP series and document rebasing or historical revisions.
6. Decide how to present external debt and exchange-rate revaluation.
7. Reconcile fiscal-year debt-service and revenue measures.
8. Establish missing-data, rounding, and revision rules.

## Reproducibility Standard

Every reported value should be reproducible from a registered source, retained raw input, documented transformation, and versioned output. Manual edits to calculated data must be avoided or explicitly logged.

## Interpretation Guardrails

- Administration shading describes time periods; it does not itself establish causation.
- Nominal debt, real debt, debt to GDP, and debt service to revenue answer different questions.
- A deflator does not remove exchange-rate revaluation from foreign-currency debt.
- Revised official series should be used consistently and disclosed.
