# Administration Boundaries

## Purpose

Define transparent rules for associating debt observations with administrations without implying that every movement was caused by the officeholder in power.

## Questions to Resolve

- Use inauguration dates, calendar-year ends, fiscal-year ends, or the nearest available reporting date?
- How should an inherited opening stock be distinguished from borrowing during an administration?
- How should partial years be presented?
- Which labels should be used for coalition or transition periods?
- Should headline comparisons use start-to-end changes, annual paths, or both?

## Decision Record

The final rule, rationale, exact dates, and sensitivity to reasonable alternatives will be recorded here before figures are finalized.
