## Definitions Requiring Resolution for KID-001

### Total public debt stock

- **Indicator ID:** `IND-DEBT-001`
- **Related glossary terms:** [[Kenya in Data/Glossary#Public debt|Public debt]], [[Kenya in Data/Glossary#Debt stock|Debt stock]], [[Kenya in Data/Glossary#Gross debt|Gross debt]]
- **Decision required:** Select one official coverage that can be compared across all four snapshots, and document any classification break.
- **Status:** proposed

### Inflation-adjusted public debt

- **Indicator ID:** `IND-DEBT-002`
- **Related glossary terms:** [[Kenya in Data/Glossary#Real value|Real value]], [[Kenya in Data/Glossary#Consumer Price Index — CPI|CPI]], [[Kenya in Data/Glossary#Deflation or inflation adjustment|Inflation adjustment]]
- **Proposed KID-001 convention:** Express the selected nominal debt stock in constant base-period KSh using a verified, continuous CPI series.
- **Decision required:** Select the exact CPI series and base period.
- **Status:** proposed

### Public debt to GDP

- **Indicator ID:** `IND-DEBT-003`
- **Related glossary terms:** [[Kenya in Data/Glossary#Debt-to-GDP ratio|Debt-to-GDP ratio]], [[Kenya in Data/Glossary#Gross Domestic Product — GDP|GDP]]
- **Decision required:** Match each debt-stock date to a compatible nominal-GDP period and disclose the convention.
- **Status:** proposed

### Domestic public debt

- **Indicator ID:** `IND-DEBT-004`
- **Related glossary term:** [[Kenya in Data/Glossary#Domestic debt|Domestic debt]]
- **Decision required:** Preserve the official source classification and determine whether it is consistent across the selected dates.
- **Status:** proposed

### External public debt

- **Indicator ID:** `IND-DEBT-005`
- **Related glossary terms:** [[Kenya in Data/Glossary#External debt|External debt]], [[Kenya in Data/Glossary#Foreign-currency debt|Foreign-currency debt]], [[Kenya in Data/Glossary#Exchange-rate revaluation|Exchange-rate revaluation]]
- **Decision required:** Preserve the official external-debt classification and explain why the KSh value can change with exchange rates.
- **Status:** proposed

### Debt service

- **Indicator ID:** `IND-DEBT-007`
- **Related glossary terms:** [[Kenya in Data/Glossary#Debt service|Debt service]], [[Kenya in Data/Glossary#Principal|Principal]], [[Kenya in Data/Glossary#Interest payment|Interest payment]]
- **Decision required:** Define whether the source measure includes principal, interest, or both and reconcile budget and financing presentations before use.
- **Status:** future work

## Approval Rule

An indicator definition becomes verified only after its institutional coverage, source continuity, unit, observation date, valuation basis, transformations, and revisions have passed the project's verification checklist.
