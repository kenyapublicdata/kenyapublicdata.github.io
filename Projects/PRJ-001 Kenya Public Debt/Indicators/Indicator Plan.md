# Indicator Plan

| Indicator ID | Indicator | Unit | Frequency | Priority | Purpose | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| IND-DEBT-001 | Total public debt, nominal | KSh | Annual or quarterly | Core | Show the commonly quoted stock | planned |
| IND-DEBT-002 | Total public debt, real | Constant KSh | Annual or quarterly | Core | Compare purchasing value over time | planned |
| IND-DEBT-003 | Public debt to GDP | Percent | Annual or quarterly | Core | Scale debt to the economy | planned |
| IND-DEBT-004 | Domestic public debt | KSh and percent of total | Annual or quarterly | Core | Show debt composition | planned |
| IND-DEBT-005 | External public debt | KSh, foreign currency where available, and percent of total | Annual or quarterly | Core | Show composition and currency exposure | planned |
| IND-DEBT-006 | Public debt per capita | KSh per person | Annual | Secondary | Show population-adjusted stock | planned |
| IND-DEBT-007 | Debt service to revenue | Percent | Fiscal year | Secondary | Show fiscal burden | planned |
| IND-DEBT-008 | Interest payments to revenue | Percent | Fiscal year | Secondary | Isolate interest pressure | planned |
| IND-MACRO-001 | GDP deflator | Index | Annual or quarterly | Supporting | Convert nominal debt into constant prices | planned |
| IND-MACRO-002 | Consumer Price Index | Index | Monthly or annual | Sensitivity | Alternative inflation adjustment | planned |
| IND-MACRO-003 | Nominal GDP | KSh | Annual or quarterly | Supporting | Calculate debt to GDP | planned |
| IND-MACRO-004 | Population | Persons | Annual | Supporting | Calculate debt per capita | planned |
| IND-FX-001 | KES exchange rate | KSh per foreign currency | Monthly or period-end | Supporting | Examine external-debt revaluation | planned |

An indicator becomes canonical only after its definition, source continuity, units, revisions, and calculation have been verified.
