# PRJ-001 — Kenya Public Debt

## Objective

Build a rigorous, reproducible evidence base for understanding Kenya's public debt over time and communicating it fairly across administrations.

## Primary Question

What does Kenya owe, to whom, on what terms, for what purpose, and what does servicing that debt mean for the economy and the public budget?

## Current Phase

**Foundation and first release** — build a small verified dataset and publish a complete introductory package before expanding the research programme.

## Long-Horizon Programme

After the launch sprint, expand incrementally into:

- a consistent annual historical series;
- debt service and fiscal burden;
- domestic/external composition;
- creditors, currencies, interest rates, and maturities;
- Eurobonds and major borrowing episodes;
- bank, pension, and investor exposure;
- debt ceilings, laws, oversight, and audits;
- use of proceeds and value for money;
- contingent liabilities and wider public-sector exposure;
- peer comparisons and sustainability scenarios.

The comprehensive map is maintained in [[Public Debt Research Programme]].

## Completion Standard for the First Release

The sprint is complete when a reader can open a stable link, understand the three comparisons, inspect the sources and methodology, download the plotted data, and independently share the figures.
