### 2026-08-27 — Baseline Debt Stock & Servicing Extraction (FY 2024/25)

- **Question:** What are the exact official figures for Kenya's current debt stock, debt-to-GDP, debt service burden, and interest vs principal split?
- **Inputs:** `TNT_Annual_Public_Debt_Report_2024_2025.pdf` (Tables 10, 13, 26, 27) and `CBK_MEI_June_2026.pdf` (Tables 7.1, 7.2).
- **Method:** Direct extraction of audited and published end-June 2025 figures and June 2026 monthly cross-checks.
- **Result:**
  - Gross Debt: **KSh 11,814.5 Billion** (53.5% Domestic / 46.5% External).
  - Debt/GDP: **67.8%** nominal (63.7% PV vs 55% target threshold).
  - Debt Service: **KSh 1,722.1 Billion / year** (71.2% of ordinary tax revenue).
  - Interest vs Principal: Interest = **KSh 987.5B** (78.6% domestic), Principal = **KSh 734.6B**.
- **Interpretation:** The acute fiscal bottleneck is not external debt, but **domestic debt interest** (KSh 776.3B), which alone consumes 32.1% of entire national tax revenues due to 14%–17% domestic borrowing yields.
