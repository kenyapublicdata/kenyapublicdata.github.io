# Research Questions

## Primary Question

How much did Kenya's public debt change across administrations after accounting for inflation and the changing size of the economy?

## Core Questions

1. What was the nominal public-debt stock at consistent comparison dates?
2. What is the same series in constant shillings using the selected deflator?
3. How did debt as a share of GDP change?
4. How sensitive is the real-debt comparison to the use of the GDP deflator versus CPI?
5. How much of the change in shilling-valued external debt reflects borrowing versus exchange-rate revaluation?
6. How did domestic and external debt shares change?
7. How did debt service change relative to revenue and expenditure?

## Definition Questions

- Which official definition of public debt is sufficiently consistent over time?
- Should the analysis use gross or net debt?
- Are guaranteed, county, state-corporation, or other contingent liabilities included?
- Which date fairly represents the start and end of each administration?
- How should fiscal-year observations be mapped to inauguration dates?
- Which GDP series and revisions should be used?

## Future Questions

- What was the debt stock per capita?
- What interest-rate and maturity risks changed over time?
- What was borrowed, and what outcomes are associated with the financed expenditure?
- How much fiscal space is constrained by debt service?
