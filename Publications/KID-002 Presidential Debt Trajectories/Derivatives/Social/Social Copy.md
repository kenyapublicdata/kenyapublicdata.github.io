### Post 1/4 (Hook & Overview)
Claiming Kenya's debt grew "300% under Kibaki vs 500% under Uhuru vs 27% under Ruto" ignores the base effect and inflation. 

When adjusted for inflation using official KNBS data (Constant 2024 KSh), the first 3 years of each administration reveal the real story 👇🧵 [Attach FIG-KID002-001_real_vs_nominal_debt_growth_mobile.png]

### Post 2/4 (Kibaki vs Uhuru)
Kibaki (Dec 2002–Jun 2005) inherited KSh 3.29T in real debt and reduced it to KSh 3.04T (📉 -7.5% real contraction). Tax collections surged, driving Debt-to-GDP down from 64.0% to 55.6%.

Uhuru (2013–2016) had the fastest real expansion (+57.5%, KSh 3.70T ➔ KSh 5.83T) driven by the 2014 Eurobond and China Exim-financed SGR borrowing.

### Post 3/4 (Ruto & The Debt Service Squeeze)
Ruto (2022–2025) added the largest nominal volume (+KSh 3.05 Trillion, +14.5% real growth). 

On Treasury's latest GDP series, Debt-to-GDP held flat (68.7% ➔ 67.8%). But debt service exploded from **47.9% to 71.2% of ordinary revenue** (KSh 1.722T), with domestic interest alone eating 32.1% of revenue at 13.0% yield.
