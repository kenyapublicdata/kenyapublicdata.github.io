# Indicator: Total Public Debt Stock (IND-PF-001)

## 1. Description & Economic Definition
Measures the gross public and publicly guaranteed debt of the Government of Kenya, broken down into **Domestic Debt** (Treasury bills, Treasury bonds, overdrafts) and **External Debt** (multilateral, bilateral, commercial Eurobonds).

## 2. Canonical Transformations
- **Nominal Debt Stock:** As reported in the National Treasury Annual Public Debt Reports and CBK Monthly Bulletins.
- **Real Debt Stock (Constant 2024 Prices):** Nominal debt deflated using the **KNBS GDP Deflator** ($Base = 2016 / 2024$).
- **Debt-to-GDP Ratio:** Nominal debt stock divided by nominal GDP for the corresponding fiscal/calendar year.
- **Per Capita Debt:** Total public debt divided by KNBS national population estimates.

## 3. Key Methodological Caveats
1. **Exchange Rate Valuation:** External debt is denominated in foreign currencies (USD, EUR, CNY, JPY). Fluctuations in the KES/USD rate mechanically increase or decrease the KSh-denominated external debt stock without new borrowing.
2. **GDP Rebasing:** Kenya rebased its GDP in 2014 and 2021. Comparisons of debt-to-GDP across historical regimes must use consistent rebased GDP time series.
